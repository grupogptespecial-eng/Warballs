# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, discuss technical decisions in good faith, avoid personal attacks and protect user privacy. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, spammy or privacy-invasive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact documented in `SECURITY.md` rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
