Libre Remote is a free and open-source remote control for LG webOS TVs.

It is designed to work from the first launch: find your TV on the local network, approve the pairing request shown on the television, and start controlling it. On later launches, the app reconnects automatically whenever possible.

FEATURES
• Direction pad with OK, Back, Home, Menu and Info
• Volume, mute and channel controls with hold-to-repeat
• Low-latency touchpad and scrolling
• Media playback controls
• Numeric keypad and colored buttons
• Installed app launcher
• HDMI and other available inputs
• Text input on the TV
• Wake-on-LAN on compatible models
• Multiple TVs with custom names and rooms
• Simple, Normal, Advanced and three custom layouts
• Light, dark, AMOLED and multiple accent themes
• Optional haptic feedback
• Network diagnostics and manual IP fallback

REAL PRIVACY
• No ads
• No account
• No in-app purchases
• No telemetry or trackers
• No external server
• Direct communication between phone and TV on the local network

COMPATIBILITY
Version 1.0 focuses on LG TVs running webOS with mobile-device control enabled. Some functions, especially powering on the TV, vary by model, webOS version and network configuration.

Libre Remote is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by LG Electronics.
